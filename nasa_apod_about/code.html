The Universe, One Day at a Time.

Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) is a NASA-led digital repository of cosmic wonders. Since its inception, it has served as a daily window into the mysteries of the universe, bridging the gap between professional astrophysics and the curious public.

expand_more

01. The Mission

Scientific Public Outreach

The primary goal of APOD is to provide a high-quality astronomical image or video each day, accompanied by a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer. It is the most popular astronomy-related site in the world, translated into over 20 languages.
